About Robo Violence 2

Robo Violence 2 is a free top-down multiplayer arena shooter. Players drive armed balls through small arenas and play deathmatch, team deathmatch and capture the flag against each other over the internet or a local network.

It continues BaboViolent 2 on a modern base, with one codebase for Windows, macOS and Linux, plus game servers hosted by us or other players.

Status: alpha. The game works with placeholder art. It plays best with your own copy of the original BaboViolent 2 data (see Installation instructions). Replacement art comes later.
