# Robo Violence 2

Free top-down multiplayer arena shooter for Windows, macOS and Linux. We continue the story of BaboViolent 2.

[Play now](https://roboviolence.com/install.md#downloads)

## Deceptively simple, fast paced

### Various game modes

Capture the flag, team deathmatch, free for all and champion.

### Windows, macOS and Linux

One game, three systems, and everyone plays on the same servers.

### Absolutely free

No in-app purchases, ads, analytics or tracking. The code is open source.

## Playing in three steps

1. **Download** The button above picks the installer for your system.
2. **Install** Follow the [install guide](https://roboviolence.com/install.md#install-steps) for Windows, macOS or Linux.
3. **Play** Choose server and [start playing](https://roboviolence.com/docs/game-manual.md).
