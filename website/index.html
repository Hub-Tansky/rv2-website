Robo Violence 2

Free top-down multiplayer arena shooter for Windows, macOS and Linux. An unofficial revival of BaboViolent 2.

Download Install guide and other platforms

Small arenas, fast rounds, no strings attached

Players drive armed balls through tight maps and fight over the internet or a local network.

Three game modes

Deathmatch, team deathmatch and capture the flag.

Windows, macOS and Linux

One game on all three systems, and everyone plays on the same servers.

Servers ready to join

Our capture the flag and team deathmatch servers are in the in-game server list. You can also run your own.

Free for good

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The code is GPLv3 on GitHub.
