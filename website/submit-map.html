Submit a map

Made a map for Robo Violence 2? Send it here. You keep the copyright. By submitting, you give the project a licence to use and distribute the map, as the agreement on this page explains. Every map is reviewed before it ships with the game.

You sign in with Discord. We receive your Discord user ID and username, not your email address. What we keep and for how long is in the privacy policy.

You need the map's .bvm file from the game's map editor, and the source and licence of anything in the map you didn't make yourself. Map submissions are for people aged 16 or older.
